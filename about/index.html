About Kairos

Cleaner communities begin with shared responsibility.

Learn about Kairos Ecocleaning and Sanitation Enterprise, our collection approach and our role in supporting a greener Ghana.

Who we are

A waste-management company focused on recovery, service and community value.

Kairos Ecocleaning and Sanitation Enterprise offers recycling services for businesses, corporations and households in Ghana. We encourage people to recycle and reduce waste pollution by rewarding positive changes in behaviour.

Clients can sort their waste, collect recyclable materials within their area and drop them at a Kairos collection point, or request pickup where available. Points are awarded according to the weight of recycled waste and can be redeemed for selected products or services.
