About Kairos

A cleaner Ghana starts with better waste habits.

Kairos Ecocleaning and Sanitation Enterprise is a waste management company offering recycling services for businesses, corporations and households in Ghana.

We encourage people to reduce waste pollution through practical collection and recycling options, supported by incentives that reward positive behaviour and help turn waste into useful resources.

Recycling rewards

Make every kilogram count.

Clients can sort their recyclable waste and drop it at a Kairos collection point or request pickup where available. Points are awarded according to the weight of recovered materials and can be redeemed for products or services.

People behind the service

Local collectors. Shared progress.

Our approach builds on trained informal waste collectors already working in neighbourhoods. By making collections more dependable and rewarding responsible recovery, the model aims to improve service for clients while supporting collector livelihoods.
