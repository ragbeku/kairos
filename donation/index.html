Our fundraising priority

A waste collection vehicle for stronger service capacity.

Kairos Ecocleaning and Sanitation Enterprise is looking for donations to acquire the waste disposal truck shown on this page. The vehicle is intended to support the collection and transportation of waste as the enterprise works with households, businesses and communities.

Contributions towards this fundraising effort will help Kairos move closer to acquiring the equipment needed for its waste-management activities in Ghana.
