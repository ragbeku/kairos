Who we serve

Reliable waste collection for the places people live, learn and work.

From households to large organisations, we tailor service arrangements to the waste volumes, collection schedules and needs of each client.

Waste collection solutions for everyone

Choose the service arrangement that fits your setting.

Collection needs vary. Kairos works with clients to consider bin size, collection frequency, sorting options and pickup arrangements.

Households

Regular collection for homes and residential properties. Clients can choose 120L, 240L or 1100L bins, customise pickup schedules, arrange weekly or on-demand pickups and separate recyclables for pickup or drop-off.

Estates & Gated Communities

Community-wide collection with customised pickup schedules and recurring weekly or on-demand service arrangements.

Businesses

Professional waste management for commercial organisations, with bin size, schedule and price assessed around operational needs.

Restaurants, Cafés & Hotels

Frequent or flexible waste collection, including discreet early-morning pickups where arranged.

Educational Institutions

Campus-wide collection, flexible scheduling and opportunities for sustainability education and recycling programmes.

Events & Venues

On-demand event collection, temporary bin placement, post-event cleanup and support during events.

Retail & Shopping Centres

Efficient collection arrangements, discreet early-morning pickups and flexible scheduling for retail settings.

Property Management

Coordinated waste solutions for multi-unit properties, including multiple locations and schedules managed through an app, as described by Kairos.
