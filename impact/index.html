Supporting informal collectors

A fleet already on your street.

Kairos builds on trained informal waste collectors already working within neighbourhoods. The approach aims to provide more dependable pickups for clients while improving earning opportunities for collectors.

Better service coordination, practical tools and access to support can help strengthen the role of local collectors in the wider waste-management system.

Waste into resources

Reducing pollution and improving recovery.

Kairos aims to reduce the prevalence of e-waste burning and unsafe manual dismantling by sourcing and responsibly processing different waste streams.

  • Reduce the unauthorised burning of electric cables.
  • Support responsible handling of refrigerators and e-waste.
  • Transform recoverable waste into useful resources and recycled products.
  • Build recycling and composting capacity that can support farming communities.
